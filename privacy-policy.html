Our Core Privacy Commitment

Fogo is built upon a fundamental architectural tenet: Local-First, Zero Remote Telemetry. We believe your browser workspace belongs solely to you. We do not track, collect, sell, rent, or transmit your browsing history, keystrokes, personal bookmarks, or search queries to any central servers.

1. Introduction & Operator Identification

This Privacy Policy governs the use of the Fogo Chrome Extension (the “Extension”) and our associated website operated by Dreainno (“we”, “us”, or “our”). This policy outlines our policies and procedures regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of information when you install and use the Extension.

By installing and using Fogo, you consent to the practices described in this Privacy Policy. If you do not agree with this policy, please do not install or use the Extension.

2. Manifest V3 Permissions & Technical Justification

In accordance with Google’s Chrome Web Store Developer Program Policies and the Limited Use Requirements, Fogo requests only the minimum set of permissions necessary to deliver its declared features:

storage & unlimitedStorage

Justification: Used exclusively to store user configurations locally on your machine, including your selected workspace layout, custom theme preferences, personal notes, and widget configurations. All data resides in chrome.storage.local, chrome.storage.sync, and client-side IndexedDB.

tabs

Justification: Required strictly for the optional “Tab Stash & RAM Saver” utility. When you explicitly trigger tab stashing, the extension reads the URLs and titles of open tabs in your active window to save them into a local session list, then closes those tabs to free system memory. This data is never sent to any external server.

alarms

Justification: Used to schedule periodic local clock updates, Pomodoro timer notifications, and cached weather forecast refreshes without running battery-draining continuous background threads.

favicon

Justification: Used strictly to fetch and display small website icons alongside your stored bookmarks within your customizable bento dashboard.

3. Information We Never Collect

To eliminate ambiguity, we explicitly state that Fogo does NOT collect, record, or transmit:

  • Your internet browsing history or visited URLs outside of your explicit, locally stored Tab Stash sessions.
  • Your IP address, geolocation, or hardware machine identifiers.
  • Keystrokes, passwords, form entries, or cookie tokens.
  • Personal biometric, demographic, or profiling data.

4. Third-Party APIs & Bring-Your-Own-Key (BYOK)

Fogo includes optional integrations with external services to enrich your dashboard experience:

  • Open-Meteo Weather Service: Weather forecasts are retrieved via non-authenticated HTTPS queries. Your broad approximate latitude/longitude is queried client-side without storing persistent location logs.
  • Generative AI Providers (Gemini / OpenAI): If you choose to enable external AI copilot summaries, you provide your own personal API key. Your API key is stored strictly within your browser’s local secure storage and queries are dispatched directly from your browser to Google or OpenAI endpoints using standard end-to-end HTTPS encryption. We do not operate an intermediary proxy server.
  • Productivity Sync (Todoist / Notion): OAuth tokens or API keys provided for task synchronization are stored locally on your device and communicate directly with the official third-party APIs.

6. Rights Under GDPR & CCPA/CPRA

Because Fogo retains your data locally on your device, you have immediate, autonomous control over your information at all times:

  • Right of Access & Portability: You may inspect and export all your saved notes, widgets, and preferences anytime directly within the Extension settings menu.
  • Right to Erasure (Right to Be Forgotten): Clearing your browser’s extension data or uninstalling Fogo immediately and permanently purges 100% of your stored preferences from your machine.
  • Do Not Sell My Personal Information: We do not sell, rent, or barter consumer personal information to third parties, satisfying all CCPA requirements.

7. Data Security Architecture

We implement rigorous security best practices compliant with Chromium Manifest V3 specifications. Fogo uses zero dynamic code evaluation (eval() is completely eliminated), enforces strict Content Security Policies (CSP), and routes all third-party requests over transport layer security (TLS/HTTPS).
