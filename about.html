The Creator Studio Story

About Fogo & Dreainno

Crafting intentional, high-performance software for mindful digital workspaces.

Our Mission

We believe your new tab should inspire calm intention, not digital chaos.

Every day, knowledge workers, creators, and developers open over 150 new browser tabs. Most new tab extensions clutter this sacred space with clickbait articles, intrusive telemetry, and heavy background scripts that slow down your computer. We built Fogo to reclaim that moment for tranquility, clarity, and deep focus.

The Origin of Fogo

Fogo was conceived at Dreainno out of personal frustration with the bloated state of productivity tools. As digital creators and engineers, our team noticed how the simple act of opening a tab often triggered immediate cognitive overload: 40 open tabs fighting for memory, disorganized bookmarks, and disjointed to-do lists scattered across multiple apps.

We asked ourselves: What if your new tab dashboard felt like an executive morning briefing at an architect’s desk? Fast, minimal, visually inspiring, and respectful of your computer’s resources. That question gave birth to Fogo.

Our Core Engineering Pillars

Extreme Performance

Engineered with native Chromium Manifest V3 APIs and hardware-accelerated CSS. Sub-millisecond cold starts, 60 FPS animations, and instant RAM stashing.

Radical Privacy

Local-first data architecture. We do not track, sell, or analyze your browsing data. Your notes, bookmarks, and preferences never leave your device.

Aesthetic Serenity

22 curated visual themes, 11 hero clock styles, and Web Audio acoustic focus soundscapes designed to induce flow states and reduce visual fatigue.

Our Business Model: Simple & Honest

Many free software products monetize by covertly selling user browsing trends or injecting sponsored affiliate links into search results. We strictly reject that model.

Instead, Fogo operates on an honest, transparent pricing structure:

  • Fogo Free Edition: 100% free forever for all core productivity modules, local tasks, clock presets, and curated aesthetic themes.
  • Fogo Pro ($9.99/year): An affordable annual pass for professionals who want advanced AI morning briefings, unlimited tab stash sessions, and continuous feature upgrades.

This straightforward model aligns our incentives directly with you: our only goal is to build software you genuinely love using every day.
